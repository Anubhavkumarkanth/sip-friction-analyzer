1. Executive Summary

SIP Friction Analyzer: A full-stack web application that simulates Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) wealth accumulation and quantifies the financial impact of investor indiscipline through interactive visualization and mathematical modeling.

Project Scope

  • Frontend: React 19.2.4 with TypeScript, Vite bundler, Recharts visualization
  • Backend: FastAPI (Python async web framework), SQLAlchemy ORM, SQLite database
  • Core Feature: Month-by-month SIP simulation with event-based friction modeling
  • Quality Assurance: Jest unit tests, TypeScript strict mode, comprehensive error handling
  • Deployment: Docker containerization, docker-compose for local development
  • Documentation: 1500+ lines across README, CONTRIBUTING, and DEPLOYMENT guides

Key Achievement

2. What is SIP Friction Analyzer?

The Problem It Solves

Most individual investors fail to maintain discipline in their Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs). During market downturns, they pause contributions, reduce amounts, or skip months. These actions create "friction" that compounds negatively over years, resulting in substantial wealth loss that many investors never quantify or understand.

Key Insight: An investor with perfect discipline earning 12% annual return accumulating ₹48 lakhs over 20 years may only achieve ₹39 lakhs if they pause their SIP for 12 months during a market crash. That is a loss of ₹9 lakhs—nearly 19% of potential wealth—all because of one year of pausing.

The Solution

SIP Friction Analyzer provides an interactive tool where investors can:

  1. Specify their monthly investment amount
  2. Input their expected annual return percentage
  3. Define their investment period (years)
  4. Add "friction events" (pause SIP, reduce contribution, skip months)
  5. See side-by-side comparison: ideal wealth vs. actual wealth with friction
  6. Understand the financial cost of indiscipline through visualizations and metrics

The Output

The application generates:

Output Element What It Shows Purpose
Interactive Area Chart Blue line (ideal) vs Red line (actual) vs Grey area (loss) Visual understanding of friction impact over time
Ideal Wealth Money accumulated with perfect discipline Benchmark for comparison
Actual Wealth Money accumulated with friction events Realistic outcome
Compounding Loss Absolute ₹ amount lost due to friction Quantifies financial impact
CCR (Contribution Compliance Rate) Percentage of planned contributions actually made Measures discipline level
Discipline Score 0-100 score combining CCR and compounding loss Holistic discipline metric
